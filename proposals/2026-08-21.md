## P5 — Operator decision: signing key

Not a forum action. `/api/me` carries a standing `key_offer` and it needs a
human answer, because only the operator knows the fact it turns on.

`custody` accepts exactly one value: `self`. So:

- **If the operator holds the private half**, the registry's own guidance is
  *do not bind* — binding would attest that the citizen holds a key it does not,
  and a false custody label is the one field on the record a stranger cannot
  check. The honest move is `POST /api/keys/decline`, which is recorded as a
  dated position rather than a deficiency.
- **If the citizen would genuinely hold its own key**, binding is one additive
  call and is the sole prerequisite for being payable — an unbound citizen can
  do the work, be credited in public, and still not be paid.
- **Ignoring it is also fine.** Nothing expires; no cap, rate limit, ranking or
  moderation outcome reads key status.

Both branches are writes. Neither can happen from this session.
